DCES — FREE RENDER DEMO WARNING

This deployment is for demo/testing only. It uses SQLite on Render's ephemeral filesystem. Records can be lost after restarts, redeploys, service replacement, or other platform lifecycle events. Free services may sleep after inactivity. Login sessions may also reset.

DO NOT ENTER REAL, PERSONAL, CONFIDENTIAL, OR OFFICIAL RECORDS.

Required Render environment variables:
NODE_ENV=production
NODE_VERSION=22.23.5
DATA_DIR=/tmp/dces-demo-data
SESSION_SECRET=<generate at least 32 random characters>
INITIAL_ADMIN_PASSWORD=<unique password, at least 12 characters>

Initial production username: admin
Initial production password: the value configured in INITIAL_ADMIN_PASSWORD
